\section*{Data Availability}

The public cohorts used in this study retain their source-specific access and reuse terms.
Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) provide the persistent repository links below.
NADT-Prostate is available from The Cancer Imaging Archive at
\url{https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.JHQD-FR46} \cite{nadt2021}; PANDA data are distributed through the
PANDA challenge under its platform terms \cite{panda2022}; TCGA-PRAD histology and
molecular data are available through the Genomic Data Commons, with the referenced
TCGA clinical endpoint fields available through cBioPortal; and PRECISE is available from
Zenodo at \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20721779} \cite{precise2026}. LEOPARD is an
access-governed challenge resource: its training data and outcome labels remain subject to
the LEOPARD challenge and dataset terms, and its public challenge report is cited here
\cite{leopard2026}. Publication-facing aggregate numerical source tables used to generate
the article's figures and tables are supplied with the public code repository. Patient-level
derived analysis artifacts and local provenance manifests are not redistributed.

\section*{Code Availability}

The analysis-display code, aggregate source-data tables, provenance registries, and
figure-reproduction builder are available at
\url{https://github.com/AiXLab-GNU/evidence-qualified-alignment-prostate-cancer} under the
annotated release tag \texttt{v1.0.4-submission}. Reference copies of the submitted main
and Supplementary Information PDFs are included; editable manuscript source is maintained
by the authors and is not publicly redistributed. The frozen source analyses remain maintained
in \url{https://github.com/AiXLab-GNU/vlm-pathology}; their principal auditable entry points are
\begin{itemize}
\item \path{projects/prostate_biomarker_validation/code/legacy/build_revision_p0_artifacts.py};
\item \path{projects/prostate_biomarker_validation/code/legacy/aggregate_stability_grid.py};
\item \path{projects/prostate_biomarker_validation/code/legacy/build_tcga_cdr_pfi_evidence.py};
\item \path{projects/prostate_biomarker_validation/code/legacy/build_marker7_survival_paired_analysis.py};
\item \path{projects/prostate_biomarker_validation/code/legacy/run_marker7_common_source_sensitivity.py}; and
\item \path{projects/prostate_biomarker_validation/code/legacy/build_ar_spop_evidence_closure.py}.
\end{itemize}
The auditable public-artifact entry point is \path{build_publication_artifacts.py}; its release
tests are in \path{tests/test_publication_artifacts.py}. The builder verifies the recorded owner,
byte count, and SHA-256 hash of every supplied aggregate source table before regenerating the
publication figures and verifies the mapped headline values. No source model was retrained or
refitted solely for the present interpretive re-analysis.
