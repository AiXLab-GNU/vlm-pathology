# Prostate biomarker validation milestones

This document refines the [governing research plan](01-prostate-biomarker-validation-plan.md)
into evidence gates. It does not modify research questions or frozen success criteria. Current
operations are recorded in the
[execution tracker](01-01-01-prostate-biomarker-validation-execution-tracker.md).

| Work package | Status | Evidence or output | Completion or entry gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Study design and protocol freeze | complete | approved designs, plans, endpoint hierarchy | analysis families and endpoints fixed |
| Target-specific analysis execution | complete/frozen | saved project outputs and manifests | source, fold, and hash reconciliation |
| Confounder, site, and stability audit | complete | nested/site summaries and correlated stability grid | uncertainty and scope retained |
| Claim–evidence integration | complete/maintained | claim-evidence matrix and endpoint hierarchy | each claim linked to approved evidence |
| Manuscript, figure, and table generation | in progress | publication bundle under `paper/` | numeric and lineage QA; author actions resolved |
| Submission-package validation | partially complete | compliance and reproducibility reports | funding, contribution, ethics, and other author-controlled facts confirmed |

milestone authority below the research plan.
