# Prostate biomarker validation survey index

Related work is a formal input to novelty, baseline, method, and claim-boundary decisions.
No repository document currently records a maintainable search protocol, inclusion/exclusion
criteria, and survey date for this project. The missing survey is therefore explicit rather
than reconstructed from manuscript prose or historical notes.

| Document | Role | Upstream input | Downstream decision | Authority status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `prostate-biomarker-qualification-related-work-survey.md` (not yet written) | transport, site/confounder qualification, and endpoint literature synthesis | [research plan](../research_plan/01-prostate-biomarker-validation-plan.md) | novelty, baseline, and manuscript related-work audit | PLANNED |

Allowed states are `PLANNED`, `CURRENT`, `SUPPORTING`, `HISTORICAL`, and `SUPERSEDED`.
A future `CURRENT` survey must be linked directly from the research plan and must record the
survey date, databases, scope, inclusion/exclusion criteria, supported research questions,
supported baseline/method decisions, and downstream documents. A `SUPERSEDED` row must link
its replacement.
