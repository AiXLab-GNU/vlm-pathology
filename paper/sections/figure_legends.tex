\newcommand{\figQualificationLegend}{%
\textbf{Qualification framework and evidence map.}
\textbf{a}, Each dot assigns one target-level decision to the strongest evidence state
supported by the frozen analyses. Transportable denotes directionally consistent
cross-cohort evidence without target-cohort refitting; context-sensitive denotes an
interpretation restricted by confounding, site, representation setting, or endpoint; and
unsupported in the frozen design denotes lack of support in the fixed primary analysis,
not biological absence. Grade and phenotype were transportable because their directions
were retained across cohorts. PTEN was context-sensitive because within-cohort support did
not establish a held-out increment beyond grade or external qualification. AR was
context-sensitive because its positive pooled direction coexisted with unresolved site
transport and no established grade-independent increment. SPOP was unsupported in the
frozen primary design and configuration-sensitive in the broader audit. PTEN is phosphatase
and tensin homolog loss, SPOP is speckle type BTB/POZ protein mutation, and AR is
androgen-receptor activity. The two recurrence rows apply different questions to the same
post-hoc Cox risk score fitted in LEOPARD and transferred to TCGA-PRAD without refitting:
transfer asks whether the score retains prognostic association, whereas increment asks
whether it adds information beyond a clinical reference model. Both recurrence decisions
remained exploratory and context-sensitive because interpretation varied with endpoint,
covariate hierarchy, or representation setting. The legacy analysis identifier was
``marker 7.'' \textbf{b}, Bar lengths count four primary decisions and two
exploratory recurrence-risk decisions; they do not encode performance, effect magnitude, or
evidentiary strength. This is not a model-performance ranking. The states summarize evidence
jointly across axes, and support on one axis does not replace evidence missing on another.}

\newcommand{\figTransportLegend}{%
\textbf{Cross-cohort grade and phenotype results.}
\textbf{a}, Spearman rank correlations for Gleason or phenotype in NADT-Prostate and for
Gleason after transfer without refitting to PANDA and PRECISE. \textbf{b}, Phenotype AUROC
after transfer without refitting to the Karolinska and Radboud PANDA subsets. Filled circles
with horizontal whiskers show the saved point estimate and patient-bootstrap interval;
open circles mark estimates for which no interval was saved. Dashed vertical lines denote
the metric null ($0$ for Spearman correlation and $0.5$ for AUROC). Labels give the saved
patient, case-image, or session denominator; unavailable intervals were not reconstructed.
Grade signals retained a positive direction in both PANDA and PRECISE, whereas phenotype
discrimination was retained in the Karolinska and Radboud PANDA subsets. Together, these
results provided the strongest cross-cohort transport evidence among the evaluated targets.
Because no uncertainty intervals were saved for the PANDA or PRECISE estimates, the
precision of their effect sizes remains unresolved.}

\newcommand{\figMolecularLegend}{%
\textbf{Patient-level molecular qualification.}
\textbf{a}, PTEN-loss and SPOP-mutation patient AUROCs in the fixed 273-patient TCGA-PRAD
frame. \textbf{b}, Androgen-receptor (AR) activity patient-level Spearman correlation in the
same frame. In \textbf{a--b}, filled circles and whiskers are frozen-primary estimates and
saved patient-bootstrap intervals; thick lines are the global minimum--maximum range across
60 correlated encoder--scale--tile--seed cells, with open circles marking their mean. These
ranges are sensitivity summaries, not patient-level confidence intervals. \textbf{c},
site-stratified SPOP patient AUROCs and patient-bootstrap intervals; site codes are retained
from the source. The cross marks CH, where AUROC was undefined because there were no positive
events among 23 patients. Dashed lines mark $0.5$ for AUROC and $0$ for correlation.}

\newcommand{\figConfounderLegend}{%
\textbf{Confounder and site audits.}
\textbf{a}, Held-out patient-level PTEN AUROC increment beyond grade for CONCH and Virchow.
\textbf{b}, Corresponding held-out AR $R^2$ increment. Both increment panels use the fixed
273-patient frame; points and whiskers are saved patient-bootstrap estimates and intervals.
\textbf{c}, Site-specific slide-level AR Spearman correlations with patient-cluster bootstrap

\newcommand{\figMarkerSevenLegend}{%
\textbf{Endpoint-conditioned post-hoc recurrence-risk transfer.}
\textbf{a}, Frozen image-risk C-index in 270 TCGA-PRAD patients for the reconstructed
with-tumor endpoint (57 events) and Official TCGA-CDR progression-free interval (PFI;
42 events). \textbf{b--c}, Same-patient, same-bootstrap-draw paired changes in C-index and
integrated Brier score (IBS; 0.5--5 years) for five model contrasts among 153 complete cases
(30 reconstructed-endpoint and 15 official-PFI events). Blue denotes the reconstructed
endpoint and orange official PFI. Points and whiskers are estimates and 95\% percentile
patient-bootstrap intervals from 2,000 valid paired draws; zero draws were undefined.
Dashed lines mark $0.5$ in \textbf{a} and no change in \textbf{b--c}. C-index change is
comparison minus reference and IBS improvement is reference minus comparison, so positive
values favor the first-named comparison model. The endpoint definitions are not equivalent.}

\newcommand{\figStabilityLegend}{%
\textbf{Decision-level stability overview.}
\textbf{a--c}, Spearman correlation, AUROC, and C-index summaries expressed relative to
their marker-specific null. Thick lines span the global minimum--maximum across 60 correlated
seed cells per marker (12 encoder--scale--tile configurations, five seeds each); open circles
mark the 60-cell mean and dashed lines the null. \textbf{d}, Percentage of the 12
configurations whose observed five-seed range straddled the null (grey) and percentage of
65 seed-matched paired contrasts whose endpoints lay on opposite sides of the null (orange).
The cells reuse patients and folds, so neither the ranges nor the null-crossing percentages
are population uncertainty estimates or independent validations. Complete grids and contrast
directions are provided in the Supplementary Information.}
