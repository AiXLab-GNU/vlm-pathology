\noindent\textbf{Figure 1.} \textbf{Qualification framework and evidence map.}
Candidate claims are connected to the evaluation dimensions that determine their evidence
state. Cross-cohort transport, confounder adjustment, site behavior, correlated setting
sensitivity and endpoint dependence are evaluated separately. Context-sensitive and
unsupported states retain their target-specific limitations.

\noindent\textbf{Figure 2.} \textbf{Cross-cohort grade and phenotype results.}
Frozen NADT-Prostate CONCH signals are shown with unrefitted PANDA transfer estimates and
the PRECISE session-level grade association. Metrics retain the saved patient, case or
session analysis unit. Missing intervals are displayed as unavailable and were not
reconstructed from aggregate estimates.

\noindent\textbf{Figure 3.} \textbf{Patient-level molecular qualification.}
PTEN loss and SPOP mutation are summarized by AUROC, and androgen-receptor activity by
Spearman correlation. Patient-bootstrap intervals describe the frozen primary analyses;
global seed-cell ranges describe correlated setting sensitivity and are not patient-level
confidence intervals.

\noindent\textbf{Figure 4.} \textbf{Confounder and site audits.}
Held-out patient-level increments beyond grade and site-specific androgen-receptor
correlations are displayed with their corresponding analysis and clustering units.
Site-specific intervals use patient-cluster resampling; these comparisons do not identify
scanner, stain or site as a cause.

\noindent\textbf{Figure 5.} \textbf{Endpoint-conditioned marker 7 transfer.}
Frozen-risk performance and same-patient, same-bootstrap-draw paired improvements are
separated for the reconstructed with-tumor endpoint and Official TCGA-CDR PFI. Positive
C-index improvement and positive integrated Brier score improvement both favor the
image-augmented model; the two endpoint definitions are not treated as equivalent.

\noindent\textbf{Figure 6.} \textbf{Decision-level stability overview.}
Global seed-cell ranges and observed five-seed-range null-straddle counts summarize
correlated sensitivity across encoder, physical scale and tile-budget settings. These
settings reuse cohorts and folds and are not independent validations; detailed grids and
seed-matched contrasts are provided in the Supplementary Information.
